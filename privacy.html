MachZero™ Privacy Policy

Effective: October 12, 2026

MachZero™ is a resale-evaluation app from Cactus🌵Byte Studios™. This policy explains what information MachZero processes when you use the website or Android app.

Information you provide

When you run an appraisal, you choose item photos to analyze. Those photos and the appraisal request are transmitted securely to MachZero's backend and to the AI service used to identify the item and produce market-pricing guidance. MachZero uses this information to provide the appraisal you requested.

App and entitlement information

MachZero creates a random installation identifier on your device. The identifier is used for scan limits, abuse prevention, paid-access entitlements, recovery, and related service operation. It is not intended to identify you by name.

Payments

The web version may use Stripe-hosted checkout for eligible purchases. MachZero does not receive or store your full payment-card number. The Google Play test build does not initiate new Stripe purchases. Existing paid access may be restored with a MachZero recovery key.

Recovery keys

If you create a paid-access recovery key, MachZero shows the readable key to you once and stores only a cryptographic hash used to verify later recovery attempts. Keep the readable key private.

Service providers

MachZero relies on service providers for hosting, AI processing, billing, and data storage. These providers may process technical request information as needed to deliver and secure their services. MachZero does not sell item photos or personal information for advertising.

Photo retention

MachZero does not intentionally store submitted item-image payloads in its application database after the appraisal request is processed. Hosting and AI service providers may process or temporarily retain request data according to their applicable service terms and security practices.

Data we do not request

MachZero does not require precise location, contacts, SMS or call logs, microphone access, health data, or broad access to your photo library. You choose the individual photos used for an appraisal.

Security

MachZero uses HTTPS for network requests and limits access to backend credentials and payment credentials to server-side systems. No system can guarantee absolute security, but MachZero is designed to minimize the personal data required for its core function.

Your choices

You can remove local MachZero data by clearing the site's/app's storage or uninstalling the app. If you need help with a privacy or data request, contact Cactus🌵Byte Studios™ through its official site.

Children

MachZero is not designed for children and is intended for a general adult resale audience.

Changes

This policy may be updated as MachZero's features, providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
